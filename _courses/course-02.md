## About this course

This course covers theories and figures in rhetoric throughout the last 2,500 years and evolution of rhetoric in communication. Persuasive communication and methodological approaches to rhetorical criticism, ethics of message creation, etc. I am taking this course as it is a degree requirement, however, I chose Professor Howell's class since I have now had him for three of my past communication courses. 

## About Professor Name

Buddy Howell has both his bachelors and masters from Baylor University, furthermore, he also earned his Ph.D. from Texas A&N University. He would then go on to become an instructor at Purdue university, and then an Assistant Professor at Denison University prior to coming to Virginia Tech. He has been an Advanced Instructor and Assistant Professor here at Virginia Tech for the last 17 years. He is a outgoing figure in the School of Communication and has a great personality. He is a great professor as well as mentor to those in his classes!

### Research interests

Buddy Howell has many skills and interests, many pertaining to communications. Some of his notable skills include: Social Networking, Public Relations, Creative Writing, Social Media, and Journalism. 

### Publications

### Service and outreach

Buddy Howell is a national, university and department award-winning teacher! He also was awarded similar awards in 2022 as well as 2020. Howell is a productive member of the Hokies and has produced a creative learning environments within his classes.
